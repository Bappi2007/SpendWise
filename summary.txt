PART 1: CORE FEATURES IN THE FIRST (BASE) VERSION OF SPENDWISE v1.0
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The initial foundation of SpendWise provided a robust, privacy-first personal finance
tracker built natively for Android using Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

1. Daily Transaction Logging & Tracking:
   - Full transaction entry: Amount, Category selection, Custom Title/Merchant with autocomplete suggestions.
   - Payment method tagging: Cash, UPI, and Card.
   - Timestamp support: Defaults to current time with custom date and time picker dialogs.
   - Optional remarks / notes field for context.

2. Grouped Transaction Feed & Interactive Actions:
   - Grouped daily timeline headers ("Today", "Yesterday", or formatted dates).
   - Daily spending totals displayed in section headers.
   - Swipe-to-delete with confirmation dialog and a 4-second interactive UNDO Snackbar.
   - Tap-to-edit bottom sheet allowing immediate editing of any recorded transaction.
   - Empty state illustration and helpful call-to-action when no transactions exist.

3. Category Management:
   - Seeded predefined categories: Food, Transport, Rent, Bills, Shopping, Health, Entertainment, Education, and Other.
   - Custom category creator: Custom name, curated icon selector, and color palette.
   - Category editing and deletion with safe reassignment: Deleting any category reassigns all past and future expenses to "Other", preserving total balances and audit history.

4. Monthly Analytics & Data Visualizations (Bespoke Compose Canvas):
   - Interactive Category Donut Chart: Custom sweep angles, center spend total, tap-to-inspect slices, and color legend.
   - Daily Spending Bar Chart: Day-by-day bar visualization across the selected month with peak spend indicators.
   - 6-Month Trend Line Chart: Smooth cubic Bezier curve displaying month-over-month trajectory with gradient underfill.
   - Key Metrics: Total month spend, month-over-month percentage change (% MoM), daily average expenditure, and peak spending day.
   - Month-over-month navigation controls (Previous / Next month stepper).
   - Top spending categories breakdown with ranked progress bars.

5. Legacy Monthly Budget Controller:
   - Fixed monthly spending limit configuration.
   - Quick increment/decrement steppers (-5k, -1k, +1k, +5k) and percentage scalers (-20%, -10%, +10%, +20%).
   - Drag slider and preset benchmark chips (15k, 25k, 35k, 50k, 75k, 100k).
   - Safe Daily Pace Calculator: (Remaining Budget / Days Left in Month) with status indicators.

6. Settings & Preferences (Jetpack DataStore):
   - Multi-currency support: Indian Rupee (INR), US Dollar (USD), Euro (EUR), British Pound (GBP), Japanese Yen (JPY).
   - Theme mode selection: System Default, Forced Light, and Forced Dark.

7. Privacy-Preserving SAF Backup & Restore:
   - Zero storage permissions: Built on Android's Storage Access Framework (SAF).
   - Versioned JSON file export (spendwise_backup_*.json).
   - Schema validation and preview inspection (shows record count, date span, total amount before restoring).
   - Two restore strategies: "Replace All Data" or "Merge (Skip Duplicates)".
   - 30-day backup overdue banner alerting the user to protect their data.

8. Spreadsheet Reports Export (CSV & Native Excel .xlsx):
   - Custom export ranges: Current Month, Custom Date Range, or All-Time.
   - RFC 4180-compliant CSV export with UTF-8 BOM (\uFEFF) for seamless opening in Excel/Google Sheets.
   - Zero-dependency OpenXML SpreadsheetML ZIP generator producing genuine dual-sheet Excel (.xlsx) workbooks:
     * Sheet 1: Formatted transactions table with formatted amounts and auto-fit columns.
     * Sheet 2: Categorized summary table with totals and percentages.
   - Instant file sharing via Android FileProvider and system share sheet.

9. Sample Data Seeder:
   - Initial utility to generate demo transactions and categories for testing purposes.

PART 2: CHRONOLOGICAL RECORD OF FEATURES & CHANGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The application evolved through a series of focused feature requests, design overhauls,
and bug fixes, recorded below in exact chronological order:

================================================================================
CHANGE 1: FRIENDS HUB & GROUP SPLIT EXPENSES
================================================================================
- Context & Motivation:
  Users needed a way to split bills with friends, track who owes them money, and visualize
  debt collections without needing a third-party server-based split app.
- Key Additions:
  * Friends Directory: Added Room `FriendEntity` and `FriendDao` allowing users to save,
    add, and remove frequent friends with auto-suggest chips.
  * Three Split Allocation Modes:
    1. Equal Division: Evenly distributes the bill among selected friends.
    2. Percentage Split: Custom percentage distribution per participant.
    3. Manual Entry: Custom exact monetary values per participant.
  * Debt & Settlement Tracking: Checkbox toggles to mark debts as "Settled" or "Pending".
  * Who Owes Me Card: High-visibility dashboard card on the home screen showing total pending
    receivables and per-friend debt breakdowns.
  * Split Expenses Visual Graph: Custom Compose Canvas chart displaying individual friend debt
    breakdowns and settled vs. pending ratios.

================================================================================
CHANGE 2: FLEXIBLE BUDGET CONTROLLER & INFLOW FUNDS TRACKER ("MY FUNDS")
================================================================================
- Context & Motivation:
  Users requested moving away from rigid, predetermined monthly caps. Instead of an arbitrary
  budget, users wanted to log real money they actually have or receive (e.g. from parents,
  freelance income, or earnings) and spend against that pool.
- Key Additions:
  * Inflow Fund Tracking: Added Room `FundEntity` and `FundDao` to log funds with amount,
    source/description (e.g. "From Parents", "Salary", "Freelance"), and timestamp.
  * Live Available to Spend Balance: Dynamically calculates:
    `Available to Spend = Total Funds Added - Month Spent`.
  * Inflow Management: View fund history, add new funds on the fly, or delete past fund entries.

================================================================================
CHANGE 3: CALENDAR-BASED DATA RANGE CLEANUP (REPLACING SAMPLE DATA)
================================================================================
- Context & Motivation:
  The user requested removing the mock sample data feature and replacing it with a

- Key Additions:
  * Removed the "Sample Data" generation buttons from Settings and Database seeders.
  * Added Calendar Range Clear: Users pick "From Date" and "To Date" via interactive
    Material 3 date pickers to permanently delete expenses within that exact span.
  * Clear All Expenses: Full wipe action that resets all expense records while cleanly
    preserving custom categories, saved friends, and user preferences.

CHANGE 5: TOGGLEABLE MONTHLY SPENDING LIMIT (ON / OFF SWITCH)
================================================================================
- Context & Motivation:
  Users requested the freedom to toggle the fixed monthly spending limit ON or OFF.
- Key Additions:
  * Added `isMonthlyLimitEnabled` boolean preference to `UserPreferencesRepository` (DataStore).
  * Integrated an ON/OFF switch in the Budget Manipulation sheet and Settings.
  * When OFF: Safe daily pace calculations and restrictive spending ceilings are disabled,
    allowing users to operate purely on their available income funds.
  * When ON: Steppers, slider, presets, and daily safe pace metrics remain active.

CHANGE 6: POST-WIPE ₹0 BUDGET RESET & HOME DASHBOARD INFLOW FUNDS FEED
================================================================================
- Context & Motivation:
  After clearing all expenses, the app previously fell back to a default ₹25,000 budget.

  and added funds should appear directly in the main dashboard alongside transactions.
- Key Additions:
  * Reset logic: After clearing expenses, when no funds are logged, available balance shows ₹0.
  * Inflow Funds in Feed: Inflow transactions (funds received from parents/earnings) now appear
    directly in the Home Screen's chronological transaction timeline.
  * Visual distinction: Inflow items display a mint green badge, "+" prefix, and source label,
    with daily headers reflecting net daily flow (Inflow vs. Outflow).

CHANGE 10: DYNAMIC INCOME-BASED MONTHLY BUDGET IN ANALYSIS SECTION
================================================================================
- Context & Motivation:
  When the monthly spending limit was toggled OFF, adding income caused the Analysis
  screen to show "Budget Exceeded!" because it was comparing spending against a 0.0 or legacy cap.
- Solution Implemented:
  * Updated `MonthSummary` (in `AnalysisModels.kt`) to include `isMonthlyLimitEnabled: Boolean`
    and `totalIncome: Double`.
  * Updated `ExpenseRepository.kt`: When `isMonthlyLimitEnabled == false`, `effectiveBudget`
    is automatically calculated as `totalIncome` (funds added during that month or all-time funds).
    `isOverBudget` is only triggered if spending exceeds actual total income.
  * Updated `AnalysisViewModel.kt`: Added `fundDao` observation into `combine(...)` so logging
    or deleting income reactively recalculates and updates the monthly summary.
  * Updated `AnalysisScreen.kt`: The Monthly Budget Card displays "Monthly Income Budget"
    and shows "Spent: ₹X" vs "Total Income: ₹Y" with positive health meters.
  * Added Robolectric unit test in `FundsAndFriendsTest.kt`:
    `when monthly limit is disabled month summary calculates budget according to total income`.

CHANGE 11: DEDICATED FRIEND MONEY LENDING & BORROWING WITH AUTO-FUND RESTORATION
================================================================================
- Context & Motivation:
  Users needed a dedicated, seamless way to track money given as loans to friends or
  borrowed from friends directly from the primary Add sheet, deduct lent money from
  available funds until repaid, and automatically restore the balance when repaid.
- Key Additions:
  * Add Sheet Mode Selector: Added a top TabRow switch ("Regular Expense" vs "Lend / Borrow")
    in AddExpenseBottomSheet with dedicated loan direction pills ("I Lent Money" vs "I Borrowed").
  * Friend Selection & Directory: Fast friend picker chips with instant selection, inline
    friend name entry, amount with currency prefix, payment method (Cash, UPI, Card), and remarks.
  * Informational Context Cards: Explains financial impact clearly in Material 3 styling.
  * Direct Money Lent Outflow:
    - Automatically creates the transaction under the dedicated "Money Lent" category (`cat_default_money_lent`).
    - Atomically creates an unpaid debt record in `ExpenseSplitEntity` linked to the friend.
    - Immediately reduces current available funds (`Available = Funds - Spent`) and tracks
      the debt inside the "Who Owes Me & Split Hub".
  * Automated Repayment & Fund Balance Restoration:
    - When the friend pays back and the debt is marked as "Settled", SpendWise automatically
      generates an inflow `FundEntity` (`id = "repay_split_${split.id}"`, title: "Repayment from [Friend]")
      which immediately adds the money back into available funds!
    - Emits a confirmation Snackbar with an interactive 4-second **UNDO** action.
    - If unmarked as paid or undone, the repayment fund is cleanly removed and balance restored.
  * Borrowed Money Inflow:
    - "I Borrowed" mode records an inflow fund entry (`FundEntity`), instantly increasing available funds.
  * Visual Badges:
    - Transactions feed on Home Screen displays dedicated "Lent to [Friend] • Awaiting Repayment"
      and "Lent to [Friend] • Repaid" status badges with the Handshake icon.
  * Automated Test Suite: Added Robolectric test in `FundsAndFriendsTest.kt`:
    `when money is lent to friend and repaid, available funds decreases then automatically restores`.
